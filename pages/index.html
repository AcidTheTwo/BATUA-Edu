About BATUA-Edu

BATUA-Edu — Balanced Asset Trading and User-Ledger Architecture for Education — recreates the core of a real trading platform: database storage, ACID trade execution, immutable ledgers, wallet accounting, lending pools and role-based administration. Prices are pulled live and clearly labelled. The goal is to encourage financial literacy. Do not consider this to be financial advice in any way, shape or form.
